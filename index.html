About REBEL SMP

The Rebellion Begins

REBEL SMP is India's most intense Minecraft Bedrock SMP — a place where alliances break, legends are born, and every login could be your last. We don't play by the rules. We make them.

From brutal PvP arenas to massive economy wars, REBEL SMP delivers a pure survival multiplayer experience driven by the community. Every season brings a fresh map, fresh chaos, and fresh legends.

Whether you're here to grind the economy, dominate the kill charts, or just survive — REBEL SMP is your battleground.
